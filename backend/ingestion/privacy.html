Privacy & data

This is a restricted sample prototype, not a private-document service.

Google sign-in

Google shares your basic identity with this app. The server verifies the signed identity token and the configured sign-in audience. This demo allows verified Google accounts, each with separate document scope. The app does not request Gmail or Google Drive permissions. Identity tokens are checked during login, not kept as access credentials.

The verified Google account identifier is transformed into a stable workspace key so the same account can retrieve its own uploads later. Your email is kept in the temporary app session to show which account is signed in. The app does not use your name or profile photo.

Your session

A secure, HttpOnly cookie identifies your session. Sessions expire after one hour, on sign-out or when the service restarts. Sign-out ends this app session; it does not sign you out of Google.

Sample documents

Uploads store original bytes, extracted text, citations and vectors in a hosted database. There is no automatic expiry or self-service delete. Removing a live record does not guarantee immediate erasure from vendor logs, history or backups. Do not upload confidential or personal information.

Hosting & contact

The app runs on Render and document records are stored in Neon PostgreSQL. These hosting providers process data needed to operate the demo. No document content is sent to a model provider in this retrieval-only version. Operators can access the database for maintenance; account isolation prevents other app users from retrieving your uploads, not operator access.

For support or a request to remove your sample records, contact hullasho7@gmail.com from the account you used to sign in. Removal is handled by an operator, not automatically, and vendor backup/history retention may still apply.

Access & limitations

Each verified Google identity has separate document and retrieval scope. Legacy shared samples are not assigned to new accounts. This is application-level isolation, not a separate physical database per user. Retrieval uses synthetic vectors, not semantic embeddings. AI-generated answers are disabled. Storage is capped at 20 documents or 5 MiB per user and 100 documents or 25 MiB across the demo. Free hosting may sleep, slow down or become unavailable.
